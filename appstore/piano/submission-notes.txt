【メモ】
No account or login is required.

HOW TO TEST
1. The 特訓 (Practice) tab opens by default. Tap「練習 開始」to start a practice session.
   The camera and microphone record the child's piano practice while the current piece
   and character cheers are shown on screen. There is no timer: tap「次へ ▶」to move to
   the next piece, and「終了」to finish.
2. After finishing, the video can be replayed in the app. The child can write what they
   want to improve next time.
3. The 積み重ね tab shows per-piece levels and note (level) progress.

PARENTAL GATE
This app is in the Kids category. The 家族 (Family) tab and sharing a video outside the
app are protected by a parental gate: a multiplication question (2-digit × 1-digit,
e.g. "23 × 3 = ?"). Type the answer and tap OK.
Three wrong answers lock the gate for 30 seconds.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Open the 家族 tab → pass the parental gate → the Premium and Family plans (monthly and
yearly) are listed there. 「購入を復元」(Restore Purchases) is on the same screen.

PERMISSIONS
- Camera: to record piano practice videos.
- Microphone: to include the piano sound and the child's voice in the video.
- Photo library (add only): to save the practice video when the parent chooses to.

DATA & KIDS CATEGORY (Guideline 1.3)
1. Third-party analytics: None. No analytics SDK; no usage, crash or behavioral data
   is collected.
2. Third-party advertising: None. No ads and no ad network SDK.
3. Data shared with third parties: Only RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com), used
   to manage auto-renewable subscriptions. Payments are processed by Apple via
   StoreKit; the app never receives payment details. RevenueCat receives only a
   randomly generated anonymous app user ID and the App Store receipt/transaction
   data needed to determine subscription status. No name, email, contact details,
   photos, videos or user-entered content. Used only for subscription status, not
   for advertising or tracking. Stored on RevenueCat's servers (United States) per
   https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. No other sharing; no data is sold.
4. Other data collection: None. Camera and microphone are used only to record the
   child's own practice; recordings stay on the device and are never uploaded.
   A video leaves the device only if a parent shares it via the iOS share sheet
   after passing the parental gate. Names, menus, notes and progress are stored
   only on the device. No accounts, no sign-in, no tracking
   (NSPrivacyTracking = false), no IDFA.
Privacy policy: https://alan-piano.pages.dev/privacy
